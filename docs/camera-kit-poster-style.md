# Security Camera Kit Poster Style

Use this visual system for all future ADE Smart Home security camera kit product posters.

## Format

- Square 1:1 ecommerce artwork, at least 1200 x 1200 pixels.
- Keep generous safe margins so the artwork works as a product card and gallery image.
- Show every included camera and recorder exactly once per listed quantity.
- Keep the equipment realistic, sharp and faithful to the supplied product references.

## Brand Direction

- Dark charcoal or black studio background.
- Warm architectural lighting and restrained stone surfaces.
- White and warm gold (`#c5a47e`) typography.
- Premium, clean and trustworthy rather than bright retail-sale styling.

## Information Hierarchy

1. ADE SMART HOME
2. Brand, resolution and package name
3. Package price in AUD
4. Exact camera and recorder quantities
5. Stock status

Use short, bold sans-serif text. Do not add installation, storage, warranty or feature claims unless they are confirmed for that exact listing.
