## The V5 Max

The V5 Max is our flagship: a push-pull smart lock with a camera doorbell built in.

1.  **Palm-vein unlock**, fast and contactless.
2.  **3D Face ID**: walk up and the door opens.
3.  **2K camera doorbell** with a 170-degree view, plus video calls and remote unlock from your phone.
4.  **4-inch screen inside** to see who is at the door.
5.  **Apple HomeKit** support.
6.  **Fingerprint, PIN, NFC card, Bluetooth key** and a backup key.
7.  **Dual batteries**, up to 6 months on a charge.
