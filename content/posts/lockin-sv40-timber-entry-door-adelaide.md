## Why the SV40

The SV40 is our value pick: it covers what most households need, without a camera or indoor screen.

1.  **Finger-vein unlock**: it reads the veins inside your finger, so it is harder to copy than a surface fingerprint.
2.  **PIN with decoy digits**: add extra numbers before or after your code so nobody can read it over your shoulder.
3.  **NFC card and app unlock**.
4.  **Fully automatic lock body**: close the door and it locks itself.
5.  **Alerts** for a door left open, low battery or unusual unlock attempts.
6.  **About 12 months of battery** (manufacturer's figure), with USB-C emergency power and a backup key.
