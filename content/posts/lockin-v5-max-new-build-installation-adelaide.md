## The Job

This family had just settled on their new build and wanted a smart lock on before they moved in. We supplied and fitted a Lockin V5 Max the same week.

![New build home in Adelaide with a tall timber front door](/img/installations/lockin-v5-max-new-build-adelaide/new-build-front-entry.webp)

## Before: The Builder's Hardware

The door came with a long pull handle and a separate deadbolt. Both came off, and the V5 Max went on in their place.

![Builder-supplied pull handle and deadbolt on the timber door before the smart lock](/img/installations/lockin-v5-max-new-build-adelaide/builder-pull-handle-before.webp)

## After

![Lockin V5 Max fitted on the full-height timber front door](/img/installations/lockin-v5-max-new-build-adelaide/lockin-v5-max-full-door.webp)

![Lockin V5 Max on the timber door beside the glass sidelight](/img/installations/lockin-v5-max-new-build-adelaide/lockin-v5-max-front-view.webp)

What the family uses it for:

1.  **Palm-vein and Face ID**: walk up and the door opens.
2.  **Built-in camera and doorbell**, so they can see who is there from their phones.
3.  **Apple HomeKit** support.
4.  **PIN, NFC card and a backup key** as well.

![Close-up of the Lockin V5 Max handle and camera](/img/installations/lockin-v5-max-new-build-adelaide/lockin-v5-max-close-up.webp)
