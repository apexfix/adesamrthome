## Features

1.  **Stores 100 fingerprints, 100 key cards and 20 PIN codes.**
2.  **Bluetooth app** to check the unlock history, send one-time codes and manage users.
3.  **Auto-lock or manual mode**, switch any time.
4.  **SafeHandle child protection**: once locked, the inside handle is frozen so little ones can't open the door by accident.
5.  **Silent mode** to mute the keypad.
6.  **8 AA batteries**, around 10 months at 10 unlocks a day.

A good fit for family homes, apartments and Airbnb hosts. We have also fitted an EasyKey bought from Bunnings, which needed [a custom CNC solution](/blog/philips-easykey-bunnings-special-cnc-solution).
