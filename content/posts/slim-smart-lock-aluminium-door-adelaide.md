## The Problem With Aluminium Doors

Most smart locks are too wide for the thin stiles on aluminium sliding and hinged doors. This door had a standard lever handle on a narrow aluminium frame:

![Aluminium glass door with a standard lever handle before the smart lock](/img/installations/slim-smart-lock-aluminium-door-adelaide/aluminium-door-lever-before.webp)

## The Fix: An Ultra-Slim Lock

We fitted an ultra-slim smart lock made for narrow frames in place of the old handle.

![Ultra-slim smart lock fitted on the narrow aluminium door frame](/img/installations/slim-smart-lock-aluminium-door-adelaide/slim-smart-lock-aluminium-after.webp)

2.  **Fingerprint.**
3.  **PIN code.**
4.  **Card.**
5.  **Slim body** that sits neatly on a narrow aluminium frame.
