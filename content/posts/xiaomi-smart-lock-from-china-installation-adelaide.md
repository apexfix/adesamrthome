## The Job

This customer bought their Xiaomi smart lock in China and asked us to install it. Locks bought overseas don't always match Australian doors, so we checked the lock body, door thickness and frame before fitting.

## What We Set Up

We set everything up in the app with the customer:

1.  **Fingerprint unlock.**
2.  **PIN code.**
3.  **NFC card or phone tap.**
4.  **Mi Home app** with alerts for unlocks, a door left open and low battery.
