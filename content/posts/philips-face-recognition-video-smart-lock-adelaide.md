## The Job

A Philips face-recognition video smart lock, fitted on a painted entry door. Walk up and the door knows it's you: no keys, no touching.

![Philips face-recognition video smart lock on a painted front door in Adelaide](/img/installations/philips-face-video-lock-adelaide/philips-face-recognition-lock-outside.webp)

## What It Does

1.  **3D face recognition** for up to 50 people.
2.  **Video intercom and smart doorbell**: a visitor presses the bell, your phone gets the alert and you can video-call them live.
3.  **100 mm HD screen inside**, so you see who is out there before you open.
4.  **Wi-Fi app** (EasyKey Plus): live view, one-time codes and the unlock history.
5.  **Face, fingerprint, PIN, card and mechanical key.**
6.  **Motion sensing** that takes photos or video when someone stays near the door.
7.  **Auto-lock or manual mode**, and rechargeable lithium batteries.
