A useful security camera system starts with the views you need, not simply the number of cameras in a box. For an Adelaide home or small business, start by identifying the entrances, access routes or stock areas that matter most to you.

## Start with two priority views

Two cameras may suit a front entrance and driveway, or a shop entrance and stock area. They will not necessarily cover an entire property. Sketch the layout and note walls, fences, trees and parked vehicles that could block the view.

Consider both the subject and the distance: seeing movement across a driveway is different from seeing detail close to a doorway. Share photos of the proposed positions before choosing the equipment. Think about light at different times of day, including glare from the afternoon sun.

## How does PoE work?

Power over Ethernet carries power and video data through a suitable network cable between each camera and a PoE connection. This is a wired setup, so planning the cable route matters even when the camera location looks straightforward.

Check whether existing cabling is suitable, where cables can enter the building and where the recorder will sit. A secure, ventilated recorder position with appropriate power and network access is worth planning before ordering.

## Leave room for future cameras

The kit's four-channel recorder provides capacity for two additional compatible cameras. Expansion still needs a compatibility, bandwidth and PoE power-budget check. Also discuss your recording needs. How many days of footage would you like to retain? Will you record continuously or around selected events? Storage requirements depend on those choices as well as resolution and recording settings. Confirm the complete equipment and setup scope in your quote.
