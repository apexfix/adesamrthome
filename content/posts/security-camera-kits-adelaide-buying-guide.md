## How does PoE work?

Power over Ethernet carries power and video data through a compatible network cable between each camera and a PoE connection. These cameras use a wired connection rather than Wi-Fi.

## Leave room for future cameras

The kit's four-channel recorder provides capacity for two additional compatible cameras. Expansion still needs a compatibility, bandwidth and PoE power-budget check. The [official Dahua recorder information](https://www.dahuasecurity.com/products/discontinued-products/network-recorders/nvr4104hs-p-4ks2/l) identifies the exact model; do not assume every camera or recorder with a similar name has identical capabilities.

Recording capacity varies with storage capacity, resolution, compression and recording settings. The number of camera channels does not specify how many days of footage can be retained.
