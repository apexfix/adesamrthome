1.  **Finger-vein unlock**: harder to copy than a fingerprint.
2.  **PIN with decoy digits**, so nobody can read your code over your shoulder.
3.  **NFC card and app unlock.**
4.  **Fully automatic lock body**: close the door and it locks itself.
5.  **About 12 months of battery** (manufacturer's figure), with USB-C emergency power and a backup key.
