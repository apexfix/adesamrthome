## The Job: A Lock the Customer Already Owned

This Adelaide homeowner had bought an eufy smart lever lock and needed someone to fit it. That is our installation-only service: you supply a compatible lock, we fit it, set it up and show you how to use it.

The front door before we started, with a standard lever handle and a separate keyed deadbolt:

![Front door with a standard lever handle and keyed deadbolt before the smart lock was fitted](/img/installations/eufy-lever-lock-adelaide/old-lever-handle-before.webp)

## What the Lock Does

The eufy lever lock gives the household two everyday ways in without a key:

1.  **Fingerprint**: the reader sits in the handle, so you unlock as you reach for it.
2.  **PIN code**: a backlit keypad for family, guests or a cleaner.

The keyed deadbolt stays on the door as a mechanical backup.

## The Result

The finished door, with the eufy lock in place of the old handle:
