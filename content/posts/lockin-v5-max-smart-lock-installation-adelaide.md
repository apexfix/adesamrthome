## Compatibility Checks for a Large Smart Lock

Before booking, we review the outside and inside faces of the door, the door edge, current lock, frame and any nearby glass. We also need to know whether an external screen door closes in front of the main door. The panel projection and handle movement must have enough clearance for both doors to operate normally.

Door thickness, mortise space and existing holes affect whether the lock can be installed as standard work. Some doors are suitable after careful preparation; others may be better matched to a narrower smart lock. Our [door compatibility check](/blog/smart-lock-door-compatibility-check) helps identify the likely path before an appointment is arranged.
