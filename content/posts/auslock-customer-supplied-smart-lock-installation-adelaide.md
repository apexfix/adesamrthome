## The Job: A Lock Nobody Would Fit

This customer had already bought an AUSLOCK smart lock and was struggling to find an installer willing to fit it. The door was an older timber door, so before booking we checked the door thickness, edge and frame from photos to confirm the lock would suit.

![AUSLOCK smart lock fitted to the edge of an older timber door in Adelaide](/img/installations/auslock-customer-lock-adelaide/auslock-smart-lock-door-edge.webp)

## What We Set Up

Once the lock was on, we set it up with the customer:

1.  **PIN keypad** for everyday entry.
2.  **Card access** for anyone who would rather tap than type.
3.  **Fingerprint unlock** for the household.
4.  **Built-in camera**, so they can see who is at the door.
