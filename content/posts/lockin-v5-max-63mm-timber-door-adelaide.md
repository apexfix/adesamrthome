## The Job

This Adelaide new build had a tall timber entry door with a glass sidelight, and the builder had fitted a basic keypad deadbolt. The owners wanted something better before moving in, so we supplied and fitted a black Lockin V5 Max.

The door is 63 mm thick, well above the usual 40 to 45 mm. A thicker door needs the right spindle and screw lengths, so we confirmed the fit from photos before booking.

![Black Lockin V5 Max smart lock on a 63 mm timber entry door with a glass sidelight](/img/installations/lockin-v5-max-63mm-timber-door-adelaide/lockin-v5-max-black-63mm-timber-door.webp)

## The V5 Max

1.  **Palm-vein unlock**, fast and contactless.
2.  **3D Face ID**: walk up and the door opens.
3.  **2K camera doorbell**, with video calls and remote unlock from your phone.
4.  **4-inch screen inside** to see who is at the door.
5.  **Apple HomeKit** support.
6.  **Fingerprint, PIN, NFC card, Bluetooth key** and a backup key.
