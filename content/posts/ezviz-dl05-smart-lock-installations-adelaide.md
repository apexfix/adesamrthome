## What the DL05 Does

1.  **Fingerprint reader** built into the handle.
2.  **Touch keypad** for PIN codes.
3.  **Card unlock**.
4.  **EZVIZ app** to manage users and see who came and went.
5.  **Doorbell button** on the front panel.
