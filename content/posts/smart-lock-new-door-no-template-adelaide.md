## The Job

A year ago we upgraded this customer's main home. They kept the old smart lock for an investment property they were building, and once the new house was finished they asked us back to fit it.

There were two problems:

1.  **No template.** The paper cut-out template was long gone, so there was nothing to mark the new door from.
2.  **Mixed-up parts.** A year in storage had mixed the long and short screws, spindles and other parts together.

## How We Handled It

1.  **Measured from scratch.** We measured the lock body and plates with calipers and marked out the cut-outs for the new door's thickness, keeping the lock and cable holes to within a millimetre.
2.  **Sorted every part.** Each screw and spindle was re-measured and matched, then tightened to an even torque so nothing presses on the motor or circuit board.
3.  **Tested by hand first.** We checked the handle and latch before setup, so it opens smoothly with no rubbing.
