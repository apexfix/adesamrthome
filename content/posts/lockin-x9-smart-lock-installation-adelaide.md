## Door Fit Comes Before Drilling

A clean result depends on more than the width of the exterior panel. Before confirming an installation, we check:

- the door material and thickness;
- the available flat area around the existing handle;
- the lock body and door-edge preparation;
- the strike position and frame alignment;
- clearance from glass panels, pull handles and security screen doors.

The photo below shows another completed X9 installation. Existing holes and lock positions vary between doors, so the same model may require different preparation on another property.
