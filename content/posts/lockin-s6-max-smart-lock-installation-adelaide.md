## Why Door Assessment Matters for the S6 Max

Camera smart locks are larger than compact lever locks. The body, mortise and indoor panel all need enough room, and the camera should have a sensible view of the approach to the door. We assess the door and frame before confirming the installation rather than assuming that one template fits every property.

The main checks include door thickness, existing cut-outs, usable panel width, mortise space, frame alignment and security-screen clearance. Glass beside the lock or an external pull handle can also reduce the available mounting area.

![Completed Lockin S6 Max installation showing the full exterior panel](/img/products/lockin-s6-max/real-install-02.jpg)

Send photos of both faces of the door, the door edge, existing lock and frame. The [ADE door compatibility guide](/blog/smart-lock-door-compatibility-check) shows exactly what to photograph. Final suitability is confirmed for the actual door and selected lock before work begins.

## Face, Vein and Visitor Features

For daily entry, Face ID can reduce the need to touch the panel, while finger-vein recognition provides another biometric method. The dual-camera system and remote video calling are intended for checking visitors at the door. The indoor 4.7-inch display provides a local view from inside the home.

These features make the S6 Max most relevant to households that want both biometric access and a visual front-door check. Customers who only need fingerprint and PIN entry may find a simpler model such as the X9 more suitable.
