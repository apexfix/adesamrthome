An Aqara D200 on a timber entry door with frosted glass panels. It's a clean, modern lock that suits Apple households especially well.

## What It Does

1.  **3D face recognition**, storing up to 50 faces: walk up and the door opens.
2.  **PIN code.**
3.  **NFC card.**
4.  **Aqara app**, plus **Apple Home** support.
5.  **Built-in doorbell.**
6.  **Backup key.**
