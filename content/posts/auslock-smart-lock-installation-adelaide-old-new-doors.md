## Careful Fitment for Existing Doors

Older doors can be more challenging than new doors because the installer needs to work with what is already there. Existing holes, worn edges, paint layers, uneven surfaces and older strike plate positions can all affect the final result.

For this AUSLOCK installation, the ADE team checked the lock position, door edge, frame alignment and inside clearance before final setup. This helped ensure the lock body was mounted securely and the door could still open, close and lock properly after the upgrade.

A professional installation should avoid the common issues that happen when a smart lock is fitted without enough preparation:

- Lock body sitting out of line
- Door needing to be pushed or lifted to lock
- Latch or deadbolt rubbing against the frame
- Existing holes being left exposed unnecessarily
- Inside lock body interfering with door trim or nearby surfaces
- Poor fitment that affects long-term reliability

## What We Check Before Installation

Before installing a smart lock, ADE Smart Home checks the practical details that decide whether the final result will be secure, neat and easy to use.

Important checks include:

1. **Door thickness**: The lock must suit the thickness and construction of the door.
2. **Door edge space**: The lock body needs enough flat area around the existing handle or lock position.
3. **Frame alignment**: The latch or deadbolt must meet the strike plate cleanly.
4. **Existing holes**: Old cut-outs may affect the new lock position or finishing.
5. **Screen door clearance**: If there is a screen or security door, the inside smart lock body must not stop it from closing.
6. **Door condition**: A dropped, warped or rubbing door may need adjustment before a smart lock is fitted.

These checks help avoid unnecessary problems and make the installation process smoother.
