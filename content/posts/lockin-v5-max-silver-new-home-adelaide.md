## What This Lock Does

1.  **Palm-vein recognition**: hold your palm up and the door opens, no touching needed.
2.  **3D structured-light Face ID**, which maps your face in fine detail, so a photo won't fool it.
3.  **2K camera with a built-in video doorbell** and a 170-degree view, so you see visitors and parcels.
4.  **4-inch screen inside** showing who is at the door before you open it.
5.  **Video calls, live view and remote unlock** from your phone, plus **Apple HomeKit** support.
6.  **Fingerprint, PIN, NFC card, Bluetooth key** and a backup mechanical key.
7.  **Dual batteries**, up to 6 months on a charge.
