## Before You Buy: Check the Door First
Most smart locks can be installed cleanly on common Adelaide timber doors, security doors and many modern entry doors. The important part is checking the door and frame before choosing the model.

A good smart lock installation is not just about the lock. The door thickness, frame clearance, existing hole position, handle style and latch alignment all affect whether the final result looks clean and locks smoothly.

## Quick Self-Check
Use this checklist before booking an installation or ordering a lock.

1. **Door material**: Timber doors are usually the easiest. Aluminium, metal, glass-framed and narrow stile doors may need a specific lock model.
2. **Door thickness**: Many smart locks need a door thickness around 35-55 mm. Thicker or thinner doors may need special parts.
3. **Frame space**: The door frame needs enough room for the strike plate and latch to close securely.
4. **Existing lock position**: If the current handle or deadbolt is too close to the door edge, glass panel or metal frame, the lock choice matters.
5. **Pull handle clearance**: Long pull handles can clash with wide smart lock bodies if the spacing is too tight.
6. **Door alignment**: If the door already rubs, drops or needs lifting to lock, it should be adjusted before fitting a smart lock.

## Doors That Usually Work Well
Smart locks usually work well on standard hinged front doors with enough flat space around the existing handle. Timber doors are the most flexible because the mortise pocket, latch hole and strike plate can be adjusted neatly.

Modern front doors with long pull handles can also work very well, but the lock body and handle clearance must be checked first.

## Doors That Need Extra Care
Some doors are still possible, but they need a more careful model choice or custom installation:

- Narrow aluminium frame doors
- Glass-panel doors with very limited lock space
- Doors with existing oversized holes
- Doors with a very narrow jamb or strike area
- Double doors and doors with unusual multipoint locking systems
- Doors that are warped, dropped or difficult to close

For these cases, ADE may recommend a different smart lock body, a custom strike plate, door adjustment, or CNC-machined hardware for a cleaner finish.
