## The Job

This household wanted one device on the front door instead of a lock, a doorbell and a separate camera. The Lockin S6 Max does all three.

![Lockin S6 Max smart lock fitted on a timber front door in Adelaide](/img/installations/lockin-s6-max-dual-camera-adelaide/lockin-s6-max-outside.webp)

## Two Cameras, No Blind Spot

The S6 Max has one camera looking straight ahead at whoever is at the door, and a second looking down so you can see a parcel left on the ground. A 4.7-inch screen inside shows who is there before you open up.

![Lockin S6 Max indoor screen showing the view outside the front door](/img/installations/lockin-s6-max-dual-camera-adelaide/lockin-s6-max-indoor-screen.webp)

## Ways In

1.  **3D face recognition**.
2.  **Finger-vein unlock**, harder to copy than a surface fingerprint and easy for kids and older family members.
3.  **PIN, app or Bluetooth, and one-time codes**, plus a backup key and optional NFC.

The app adds live view, video calls and remote unlock, motion snapshots, and alerts for low battery, forced entry, someone lingering at the door or a door left unlocked.
